# Letterhead

The printed first page for letters, proposals and SOWs: wordmark with motto top left, contact block top right, seal in the footer, key band along the bottom edge.

**The consumer provides:** the letter body, and the contact details (street address, ZIP, email) still shown as placeholders.

- US Letter, 0.75in (`print-margin`) side margins, white stock (`paper-print`) by default.
- Header: `wordmark-motto.png` at about 2.6in wide; contact block right-aligned in `print-small`, company name semibold in `ink`.
- Body in `print-body`. In Word, the body falls back to Calibri unless Plus Jakarta Sans is installed.
- Footer: `seal.png` at 0.65in, then a `rule` line over "Mission-built software for higher education and publishing" and "Brooklyn, NY".
- Bottom edge: `key-band.png`, full bleed, 8px (1/12in) tall.
- Continuation pages use the small `wordmark.png` (no motto), a running head with recipient, date and page number, and the same footer band without the seal.
