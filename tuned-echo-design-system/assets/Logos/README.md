# Logos

Transparent PNGs cut from the original logo artwork. They are images, so they look right in Word, slides and email without the fonts installed.

- `wordmark.png`: wordmark and key strip, ink `#1f2329`. Default for documents, slides and email signatures on light grounds.
- `wordmark-motto.png`: wordmark, strip and the motto "We tune your systems until they echo your mission." Letterhead, report covers, title slides. Use at 2.3in wide or more so the motto stays legible.
- `wordmark-reversed.png`: wordmark and strip with cream `#f6f2ea` ink and a cream seventh bar, for `ink` grounds.
- `seal.png`: the seal in ink `#1f2329`. Footers, back covers, closing slides, favicons.
- `seal-reversed.png`: the seal in cream `#f6f2ea`, for `ink` grounds.

The website draws the seal as vector art (`assets/seal.svg` in the tunedecho.github.io repository), using `currentColor` and Oswald lettering.
